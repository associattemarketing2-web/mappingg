**Date checked:** 13 Oct 2026 (IST). Research only. Nothing was bought, no vendor was contacted, and no ads were launched. No website code, database or settings were changed, and no dependencies were added to the project (the workbooks were built from a temporary workspace).

## Key findings

1. **No provider sells a ready list of 500 consented ₹1 Cr+ buyers.** The only routes that can reliably reach 500 are **your own campaigns**: Google Search to a landing page or lead form, and Meta Lead Ads. With those, the buyer types or confirms their number under consent wording that you write.
2. **Portals (99acres, Magicbricks, Housing.com)** give you responses on your listings. Volume isn't guaranteed (99acres says so itself), prices are quote-only, and the portals blocked automated reading of their pages. None of their documents that we could read covers using responders for promotional calls about other projects.
3. **NoBroker** is built for owners selling their own home. Its seller plans cost ₹7,499–20,999 + GST, and it isn't a lead source for brokers.
4. **Data brokers, scraped data and wealth/celebrity lists** are category D. Don't use them.
5. **All cost figures are illustrative.** The CPLs come from two vendor benchmarks, and every rate is an editable assumption. Run a 2–3 week test to get real numbers.

## Illustrative budget (base scenario, incl. an assumed 18% GST)

| Target | Google Search | Meta Lead Ads |
|---|---|---|
| 500 raw leads | ≈ ₹7.7 L | ≈ ₹5.3 L |
| 500 qualified ₹1 Cr+ buyers | ≈ ₹29.6 L | ≈ ₹35.7 L |
| 500 call-ready (qualified + call consent) | ≈ ₹39.5 L | ≈ ₹54.9 L |

The full conservative-to-optimistic ranges are in the workbook. In short, raw leads are cheap and qualified, consented ₹1 Cr+ buyers are not.

## Before any of this goes live

- Fix the consent capture on the website (pre-ticked map box, hardcoded `consent: true`, WhatsApp-only sign-up wording). That is a separate code task that needs your approval.
- Put your MahaRERA agent registration number, and a project QR code where needed, on ads.
- Set up a registered 140-series line or telemarketer, scrub numbers against DND, and get a legal review of DPDP Rules timing.

## Limitations

- Portal pricing and terms couldn't be read directly. Get them confirmed by written quote.
- The Meta lead retention period (~90 days) comes from third-party guides.
- Whether Google lead forms accept a custom consent question isn't confirmed.
- Property expos weren't researched in detail.
- Legal points come from secondary sources and need a lawyer's confirmation.
