## Limitations

- Phones and emails are the **company's** published office contacts. No direct lines or personal emails were found on the official pages read, and none were guessed from name patterns.
- A person's Pune connection is the company's Pune head/registered office shown on its official site. Roles that name another region (e.g. "Head – Africa") are in the review file.
- Email checks are syntax + MX only. No test emails were sent, so no mailbox is "delivery confirmed".
- The IBJA directory gives name, firm and city only. Members are exported only when their firm's official website supplies a contact, and their role stays "not stated".
- LinkedIn, Google Maps, Justdial/IndiaMART and the JBO directory were not accessed (terms or login). The web-search adapter needs `BRAVE_SEARCH_API_KEY` or `GOOGLE_CSE_KEY` + `GOOGLE_CSE_ID`.
- Designations change. Re-run before outreach and check `last_verified_at`.
- Contacting these people is subject to India's DPDP Act, TRAI DND rules and anti-spam law; `contact_permission_status` records that no individual consent exists.
