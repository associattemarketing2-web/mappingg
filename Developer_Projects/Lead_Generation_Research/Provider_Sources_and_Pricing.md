# Provider Sources and Pricing

**Date checked:** 13 Oct 2026 (IST). **Method:** web search and official help pages only. Nothing was bought, no sales team was contacted, and no login or CAPTCHA was bypassed.

## Read this first

- **Magicbricks, 99acres and Housing.com block automated fetching.** Magicbricks also blocks the search tool's crawler. Their official pricing pages could not be read directly, so every price below comes from a search snippet of the official page or from a third-party source, and is labelled that way.
- **"Quote required"** means no official public price was found. Ask the official sales channel for a written quote.
- **CPL figures for Meta/Google are third-party vendor benchmarks, not published prices.** Both sources sell lead-generation services. Use them only as planning ranges.
- Prices exclude GST unless stated.

## Indian law and policy (flag for legal confirmation)

| Topic | Finding | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| DPDP Act 2023 + Rules 2025 | Rules notified 13/14 Nov 2025, phased over ~18 months (full effect ~13 May 2027). Notice/consent obligations are reported in a later tranche, **sources disagree on the exact dates**. Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, with clear affirmative action (Act s.6) | [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-brought-into-force-), [TCSA roadmap](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/dpdp-rules-2025-implementation-roadmap) | Medium. **Legal confirmation needed** |
| TRAI TCCCPR (2025 amendment) | Commercial calls must use registered headers / designated series. **140-series for promotional calls**, 1600-series for service/transactional (1600 mandated for RBI/SEBI/PFRDA entities). Promotional callers must register with telecom providers. Penalty and complaint-threshold details come from secondary sources | [TRAI PR 11/2025](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf) (server refused our connection), [JSA summary](https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/JSA-Prism-Telecommunications_February-2025_TCCCPR-amendments.Final_.pdf), [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/?p=390749) | Medium. **Legal confirmation needed** |
| DND | Promotional calls/SMS to DND-registered numbers need recorded consent. Our site's "consent overrides DNC" wording is not a substitute for a compliant consent record | Same | Medium |
| WhatsApp Business | Opt-in required before template messages. The opt-in must name the business and say what the person is agreeing to. Opt-outs must be honoured. Meta recommends separate opt-ins per message category | [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy), [Get opt-in](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in) | High |
| MahaRERA (agents) | Agents must be registered. The registration number, plus a project QR code where a project is promoted, must appear in ads, with font-size rules (Apr 2025 circular; penalty up to ₹50,000) | [The Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/regulatory/maharera-makes-qr-code-registration-number-mandatory-in-ads-50000-penalty-for-violations/) | Medium. Confirm on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in |
