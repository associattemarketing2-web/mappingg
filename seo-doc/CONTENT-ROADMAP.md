# Content roadmap — 90 days from 4 Nov 2026

Rules for every piece: answers one real buyer question; numbers come from live Mappingg data
(generated by `migration/seed-blogs.mjs`, never typed by hand) or from an official source that is
linked; shows published and updated dates; written and reviewed by the Mappingg team; no invented
experts, ratings or statistics. One primary keyword per article, different from every listing page
(see KEYWORD-RESEARCH.md §4).

## 3. What not to publish
- Generic AI-written city overviews without data.
- Pages for localities with < 3 projects, or BHK/budget combinations with < 6.
- Investment “returns” claims, rental yields or price forecasts without a citable source.
